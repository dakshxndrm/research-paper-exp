[IPv4/IPv6 dual-stack](/classification/kubernetes_cluster_networking_types.md) networking is enabled by default for your [Kubernetes cluster](/definition/kubernetes_cluster_architecture.md) starting in 1.21, allowing the simultaneous assignment of both IPv4 and IPv6 addresses.
