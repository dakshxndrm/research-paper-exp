A [Kubernetes cluster](/definition/kubernetes_cluster_architecture.md) consists of several key components, including the [API server](/policy/api_server_behavior_in_kubernetes.md), [controller](/pattern/kubernetes_controller_pattern.md) manager, scheduler, and worker nodes. Each component plays a crucial role in managing and running containerized applications.
