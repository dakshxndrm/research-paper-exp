[Kubernetes](/policy/garbage_collection_in_kubernetes.md) is a container orchestration system for automating the [deployment](/entity/deployment.md), scaling, and management of containerized applications. It was originally designed by Google, and is now maintained by the Cloud Native Computing Foundation.
