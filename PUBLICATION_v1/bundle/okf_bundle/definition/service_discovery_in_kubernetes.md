In [Kubernetes](/policy/garbage_collection_in_kubernetes.md), [service discovery](/list/kubernetes_service_discovery.md) refers to the [process](/concept/pod_definition.md) of automatically discovering and registering [services](/procedure/configuring_load_balancing_and_services.md) within a cluster. This allows applications to communicate with each other without needing to know their IP addresses or ports.
