In [Kubernetes](/policy/garbage_collection_in_kubernetes.md), a Pod represents a set of one or more running containers on your cluster. [Pods](/definition/kubernetes_cluster_architecture.md) have a defined [lifecycle](/policy/podgroup_ownership_and_lifecycle.md) and can be managed by [workload](/entity/workload.md) resources.
