The [Workload API](/entity/workload_api.md) allows you to define [PodGroupTemplates](/template/podgrouptemplates.md) to group [Pods](/definition/kubernetes_cluster_architecture.md) and apply advanced [scheduling policies](/policy/scheduling_policies_in_kube_scheduler.md) to them, such as [gang scheduling](/procedure/gang_scheduling.md).
