In [Kubernetes](/policy/garbage_collection_in_kubernetes.md), a [namespace](/definition/api_group_resource_type_namespace_and_name.md) is a logical partitioning of resources within a cluster. It allows multiple applications to share the same cluster without conflicts or [resource](/resource/cluster_resources.md) contention.
