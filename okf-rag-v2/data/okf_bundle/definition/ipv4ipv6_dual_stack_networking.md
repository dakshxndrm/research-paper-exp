IPv4/IPv6 dual-stack [networking](/definition/application_exposure_service_and_ingress.md) enables the allocation of both IPv4 and IPv6 addresses to Pods and Services. It is enabled by default for Kubernetes clusters starting in version 1.21, allowing simultaneous assignment of both address families.
