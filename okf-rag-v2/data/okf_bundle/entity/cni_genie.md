CNI-Genie enables Kubernetes to seamlessly connect to a choice of [CNI plugins](/definition/external_components_implementing_kubernetes_networking.md), such as [Calico](/entity/calico.md), Canal, Flannel, or Weave. It is a CNCF project at the Sandbox level.
