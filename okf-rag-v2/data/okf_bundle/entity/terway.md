Terway is a suite of [CNI plugins](/definition/external_components_implementing_kubernetes_networking.md) based on AlibabaCloud's VPC and ECS network products. It provides native VPC [networking](/definition/application_exposure_service_and_ingress.md) and [network policies](/definition/network_policies.md) in AlibabaCloud environments.
