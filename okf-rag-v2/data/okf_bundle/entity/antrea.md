Antrea operates at Layer 3/4 to provide [networking and security](/concept/networking_and_security.md) services for Kubernetes, leveraging Open vSwitch as the [networking](/definition/application_exposure_service_and_ingress.md) data plane. Antrea is a CNCF project at the Sandbox level.
