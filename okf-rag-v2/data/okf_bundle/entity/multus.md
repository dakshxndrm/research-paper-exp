Multus is a Multi plugin for multiple network support in Kubernetes to support all [CNI plugins](/definition/external_components_implementing_kubernetes_networking.md) (e.g. [Calico](/entity/calico.md), [Cilium](/entity/cilium.md), [Contiv](/entity/contiv.md), Flannel), in addition to SRIOV, DPDK, OVS-DPDK and VPP based workloads in Kubernetes.
