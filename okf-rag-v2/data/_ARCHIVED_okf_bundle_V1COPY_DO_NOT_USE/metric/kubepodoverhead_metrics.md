Some `kube_pod_overhead_*` metrics are available in [kube-state-metrics](/list/legacy_and_other_kubernetes_add_ons.md) to help identify when [Pod overhead](/policy/pod_overhead_accounting.md) is being utilized and to help observe stability of workloads running with a defined overhead.
