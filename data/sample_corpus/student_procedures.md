# Student Procedures Handbook

## Medical Exemption Process

A student seeking a medical exemption from the attendance requirement must
submit Form MX-2 along with a certificate issued by a registered medical
practitioner holding an MBBS degree or higher.

The application must be submitted within 10 working days of returning to
classes. Applications submitted later are rejected without review.

The certificate is verified by the Institute Medical Officer, who forwards a
recommendation to the Dean of Academics. Final approval of a medical exemption
rests solely with the Dean of Academics. The Head of Department may comment on
the application but cannot approve it.

An approved exemption raises the student's effective attendance to exactly 75
percent for the affected course. It does not raise it beyond that threshold and
does not apply retroactively to a previous semester.

## Change of Specialisation

A final-year student may apply once to change their declared specialisation.
The application requires the joint approval of the Head of Department and the
Dean of Academics. Either authority may reject the application independently.

The change is permitted only if the student has cleared all courses of the
previous specialisation with no pending backlogs.

## Grievance Redressal

A student dissatisfied with any academic decision may appeal to the Grievance
Committee within 21 days of the decision being communicated. The Grievance
Committee is chaired by a faculty member nominated by the Director and cannot
include the Dean of Academics.
