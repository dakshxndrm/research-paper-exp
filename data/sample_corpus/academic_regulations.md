# Academic Regulations — Undergraduate Programmes

## Attendance

Every student must maintain a minimum of 75 percent attendance in each
registered course to be eligible to sit for the end-semester examination.
Attendance is computed from the first instructional day of the semester to the
last, and is recorded per course, not as an aggregate across courses.

A student falling below 75 percent in any course is debarred from the
end-semester examination for that course and is awarded a grade of DB.

Students who fall between 65 and 75 percent may apply for a medical exemption.
The exemption is not automatic and is subject to the medical exemption process.

## Examination Passing Criteria

For a theory course, a student must obtain at least 40 percent of the total
marks to be declared as having passed the course.

For a laboratory course, a student must obtain at least 40 percent in the
practical component and at least 40 percent overall. Failure in the practical
component alone results in failure of the entire course, regardless of the
overall total.

## Registration and Late Fees

Course registration opens two weeks before the semester begins and closes on
the last working day before instruction starts. Registration submitted after
the deadline attracts a late fee of 500 rupees per day of delay, capped at a
maximum of 5000 rupees.

A student who has not registered within 15 days of the deadline is deemed to
have withdrawn from the semester.
