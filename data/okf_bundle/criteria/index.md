# criteria

- [Laboratory Course Passing Requirements](laboratory_course_passing_requirements.md) — Laboratory courses require a minimum of 40 percent in both the practical component and overall marks; failure in the practical alone results in overall failure.
- [Theory Course Passing Threshold](theory_course_passing_threshold.md) — A theory course is passed when a student obtains at least 40 percent of the total marks.
